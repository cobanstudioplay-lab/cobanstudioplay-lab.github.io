# Privacy Policy — Arrow Tower 3D: Puzzle Escape

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

Arrow Tower 3D: Puzzle Escape ("the game") is developed by COBAN STUDIO PLAY ("we"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play.

**Information we collect directly.** None. The game has no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts. Your progress (level, hints, sound and language settings) is stored only on your device.

**Advertising (Google AdMob).** The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

- your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
- your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
- interaction data (for example app launches, taps and ad views);
- diagnostic information (for example crash logs and performance data).

This data is encrypted in transit and handled under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and can change their choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

**Deleting data.** Uninstalling the game deletes all data stored on your device. For questions or requests, contact us.

**Contact:** cobanstudio.play@gmail.com
